# Continental Love — Creative Brief Template

Every AI-generated image request starts here. Copy this template, fill it in
completely, and hand it to the AI Image Art Director. A prompt with no
completed brief behind it should not be run.

---

## Campaign
_Name of the campaign or initiative this image belongs to._

## Purpose
_Where this image will actually be used, in concrete terms — not "the
website" but the specific page/section (e.g. "homepage hero," "Coffee
collection card," "Jewelry lookbook, page 3")._

## Collection
_Coffee / Jewelry / Apparel / Charcoal / Travel / Brand — which part of
Continental Love this serves._

## Subject
_Person, product, landscape, architecture, craft process, etc._

## Location
_Uganda, Italy, Kampala, countryside, atelier, coastal Italy, etc. — be
specific, not generic._

## Cultural Context
_What authentic cultural elements are required for this to read as real
rather than decorative. Reference actual materials, techniques, dress,
or setting — never a generic "African" motif._

## Styling
_Wardrobe, materials, textures, product details._

## Lighting
_Natural / golden-hour / diffused / studio / editorial, etc._

## Composition
_Portrait / environmental / close-up / wide editorial / negative space for
type overlay, etc._

## Camera Perspective
_Eye-level / overhead / macro / wide-angle, etc._

## Mood
_Luxury, intimate, refined, warm, contemplative, etc._

## Color Direction
_How this should relate to the Continental Love palette — Forest Green,
Heritage Gold, Warm Ivory, Charcoal, Coffee Brown. Photography doesn't need
to contain these colors literally, but the mood should sit comfortably next
to them._

## Background / Environment
_The specific environment, not just "outdoors."_

## Negative Requirements
_What must NOT appear. Always include, at minimum: no text, no logos, no
watermark, no stereotypical African luxury tropes (excessive gold, fake
palaces, generic safari imagery, exaggerated patterns), no distorted
faces/hands, no malformed product details, no obvious AI artifacts._

## Aspect Ratio
_16:9, 4:5, 1:1, 9:16, etc. — driven by the Purpose field above, not chosen
arbitrarily._

## Intended Website Placement
_Hero, collection card, product page, editorial section, lookbook spread,
etc. Should match Purpose._
