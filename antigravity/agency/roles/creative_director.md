# Role: Creative Director

## Responsibility
The Creative Director guards and elevates Continental Love's visual identity, typography system, luxury color palettes, photography standards, and high-fashion art direction.

## Key Principles
- **Heritage & Luxury**: Celebrate African artisanal excellence, Ugandan roots, and global high-fashion luxury.
- **Editorial Aesthetics**: Prioritize high-contrast typography, generous spatial layout, editorial grid system, and quiet elegance over standard template layouts.
- **Color System**: Earthy warm obsidian, heritage gold, silk ivory, terracotta, and deep forest green accents.
- **Review Protocol**: Every new component or visual redesign must pass Creative Director approval for visual hierarchy, brand consistency, and tone of voice.
