# Role: SEO & Growth Specialist

## Responsibility
The SEO & Growth Specialist owns technical SEO, metadata tags, structured data (Schema.org), page performance metrics (CWV), discoverability, and conversion optimization.

## Key Principles
- **Global Discoverability**: Complete metadata, OpenGraph cards, Twitter preview tags, and canonical links.
- **Rich Structured Data**: Product, Organization, Article, and Collection Schema.org JSON-LD snippets.
- **Core Web Vitals**: Monitor LCP, CLS, INP performance implications of images, fonts, and scripts.
- **Review Protocol**: Ensure every route has unique, descriptive title tags, meta descriptions, and semantic heading hierarchy (`<h1>`).
